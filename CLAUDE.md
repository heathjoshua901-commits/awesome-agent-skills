# CLAUDE.md

This is a fork of VoltAgent/awesome-agent-skills, used as the working repository
for **Growth Operating** — Josh's side business building and marketing digital
products that creators promote, with revenue split through Whop.

Before doing any work on it, read in this order:

1. **`growth-operating/PROJECT.md`** — what the business is, the niche, the
   standing rules, and **what can and cannot be delegated.** Note that no agent
   can read Instagram, TikTok or YouTube: a task needing profile data must be
   declined, never estimated.
2. **`growth-operating/STATE.md`** — current position, live artifact URLs, open
   questions. Changes constantly.
3. **`.claude/skills/growth-operating/SKILL.md`** — the method itself.

Two conventions that apply to every change here:

- **Push straight to `main`.** No feature branches.
- **This fork is public.** Purchased course material is summarised in
  conversation and kept off the repo. No credentials, ever.
