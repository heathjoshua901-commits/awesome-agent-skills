## Sourcing job before screening

The list below came from one web search sweep, which clustered small. **Find micro
accounts (10k–100k) in this niche before working the list.**

Do it in the Instagram app, using the three search shapes and the ready-made query
set in `references/sourcing.md`. Note the accounts that recur across more than one
query. Keep the owner's question, not the customer's — "how to price salon
services" returns coaches, "how to get balayage to last" returns salons
advertising to clients.

The productised peer method still applies when working from the web: take an
incumbent from the market-proof table and find who else teaches the same
audience.

## What to record per screen

Tier and follower count · audience composition (owners or consumers) · existing
product and price · two verbatim comments naming a problem · posting cadence ·
pre-screen score out of 8.

**The four opportunity indicators**, which are indicators and not requirements:
engagement (comments, not likes) · a clear lane with a specific angle within it ·
consistent posting · monetisation signals (brand or affiliate links in bio, a
business email for enquiries).

**The gap, in one sentence.** What would this audience pay for that this creator
is not currently offering them? If that sentence cannot be written, there is no
deal here however good everything else looks.

**The swap test:** cover the handle and read your notes. If they would fit any
other coach in the segment, you have not found the anchored detail yet.

**Before any outreach, search the creator's name by hand.** The platform analysis
has under-read a creator's product range twice. A named product is reliable when
it appears; its absence proves nothing.
