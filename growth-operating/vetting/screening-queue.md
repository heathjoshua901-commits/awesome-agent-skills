so the only cost is the time to paste a handle. Work down the list and move
anything that survives into its own vetting memo.

**Nothing here is verified.** Follower counts, whether they already sell, and
audience quality are all unknown until screened. The list is an input, not a
shortlist.

## What to record per screen

Follower count · existing product named or none · highest-rated theme · the
verdict's proposed offer · whether it passes the swap test.

The swap test: cover the handle and read the analysis. If it would fit any other
account in the same genre, it is filler. One anchored detail specific to that
person is what makes it real.
