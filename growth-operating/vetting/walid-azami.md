## Why skipped

The briefing excludes "anyone whose bio links to a paid course or product." Azami
is productised on two fronts, both verified:

- **S.T.E.P. Pricing Course.** Promoted inside the post body of
  [Don't Lower Your Photography Rates!](https://walidazami.substack.com/p/dont-lower-your-photography-rates)
  (2 Sep 2024), fetched directly.
- **Visual Business Academy**, a private paid Discord community offering
  mentorship to photographers and videographers, with free and paid tiers.

He is the strongest niche fit found in the whole sweep — pricing, rates, business
positioning, aimed squarely at photographers trying to earn. He is also exactly
the creator profile the filter is designed to exclude, because he has already
built and sold the product a growth operator would otherwise build with him.

## Evidence not obtainable

Three verbatim audience comments could not be recorded. Substack post pages
render the article body but the discussion section returned "No posts" on fetch;
comments are not in the served HTML. Subscriber count is not shown on either the
publication homepage or the `/about` page.

## Note for a later sweep

If the filter is ever relaxed to "productised but under-monetised," he is the
first name to revisit. An existing course plus a paid community is proof of
buying intent, not an absence of it — it changes the deal shape from build-a-
product to scale-an-existing-one.
