# Vetting: owner-operated local service businesses

Niche chosen 14 September 2026. Scorecard and gates:
`.claude/skills/growth-operating/SKILL.md`. Method, tiering and the pre-screen
bar: `references/sourcing.md`.

The partner is **an educator whose audience is owners** of salons, spas, med
spas, barbershops, gyms or studios. Not the businesses themselves.

## Nothing here is verified

Handles and follower counts below come from web search result metadata. They are
a starting list, not a shortlist. Follower counts, audience composition, comment
quality and existing products are all unknown until Josh screens them on
Instagram, which is the only readable surface.

`screening-queue.md` is the working list. Each screened creator gets a memo in
this directory.

## Route

**Route two.** Every Tier A candidate in this niche already sells something, and
that is the point rather than a problem: the demand is proven by revenue before
a single message is sent. The deal is a share of the lift on an existing product,
not a new product built from nothing.

The asset built before outreach goes **in front of** what they already sell, and
must not duplicate it. Establish the baseline first — what they sell, the price,
how they sell it, what it covers — per `references/outreach.md`.
