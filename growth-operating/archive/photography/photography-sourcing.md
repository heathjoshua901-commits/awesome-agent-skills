# Photography creator sourcing sweep

Stage 1 of the pipeline, run for the photography niche.

**Discovery is on-platform, not in ListKit.** ListKit sells triple-verified B2B
contact data on decision-maker profiles. It can enrich and hold a creator record
once you have a name, but it does not index creators by comment quality or
audience behaviour, which is what Gate 1 scores. Find candidates by searching
the platform the creator actually posts on, then push the record into ListKit
for contact data and pipeline tracking.

## Sort by whose audience it is, before anything else

Photography is a large-audience, low-buying-intent niche by default. The people
who pay are photographers running or trying to run a business. People who enjoy
photographs do not buy. Follower count is the least informative number on the
page here.

### Tier A — source these

Audience is working or aspiring photographers with a revenue problem.

- Photography business, pricing, and client-acquisition educators. A market
  already clearing $250 for a single consult up to $12,000 for a year of
  coaching.
- Real estate and commercial property photography operators. Roughly $200–500
  per shoot, systems-driven, and a steady flow of new entrants who need to be
  taught the workflow.
- Wedding photography business educators. The service itself is saturated and
  seasonal; the education sold around it still moves.
- Editing and retouching workflow (Lightroom, Capture One) where the audience is
  delivering paid client work rather than editing holiday photos.

### Tier B — conditional

- Preset and aesthetic creators. Proven buyers, but at $19–49. Typical part-time
  preset sellers report $100–500/month. The audience has bought once; the open
  question is whether it carries a $200+ ticket. Shortlist only with evidence of
  a higher-priced sale or an email list.

### Tier C — skip, do not score

Gear reviews and camera news, travel and landscape inspiration, street
photography art accounts, phone photography tips. These audiences consume, they
do not buy outcomes. They score 0 on Buying intent, which fails Gate 1 outright
no matter how large the following.

## Gate 1 cannot be completed at stage 1

Only four of the six signals are visible before the creator replies:

| Gate 1 signal | Visible pre-contact | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| Audience trust | Partly | Do commenters ask the creator questions, and does the creator answer? |
| Buying intent in niche | Yes | Tier above, plus whether comments ask about clients, pricing, or gear-as-purchase |
| Engagement quality | Yes | Last 20 posts' comments |
| Existing monetisation | Yes | Bio links: Whop, Gumroad, Kajabi, Teachable, a newsletter signup |
| Responsiveness | No | Only after you message them |
| Content consistency | Yes | Posting cadence over the last 6 months |

The skill's pass bar is 8 of 12. Four observable signals cap out at 8, so a
public-data pass is not possible and should not be faked.

**Pre-screen bar:** score the four observable signals out of 8. Shortlist at 5
or more with no 0 on Audience trust or Buying intent. Complete the full 12-point
score after the first reply. Responsiveness is unscoreable until they respond,
and it is the signal that eliminates most otherwise-strong candidates.

## Per-candidate pre-screen, about 10 minutes

1. Tier check. Tier C is dropped without scoring.
2. Read the comments on the last 20 posts. Record two verbatim comments that
   name a problem. If there are none, Engagement quality is 0.
3. Open every bio link. Note any existing product and its price.
4. Check posting cadence across 6 months.
5. Write the memo. No memo, no stage 3 — this is the skill's standing rule.

## Photography-specific red flags

On top of the standing red flags in the skill:

- Bio links to an affiliate gear storefront and nothing else. The audience buys
  cameras, not outcomes.
- Portfolio-only account with no teaching posts. Nothing to build an offer from.
- Comments are overwhelmingly emoji or "what lens is that". Gear curiosity is
  not buying intent for education.

## Pre-screen memo

```
Creator:
Platform and handle:
Tier: A / B / C
Pre-screen score: X/8   (Audience trust, Buying intent, Engagement quality, Existing monetisation)
Evidence per signal:
Two verbatim audience comments:
Existing product and price:
Red flags:
Decision: contact / skip
The one problem their audience keeps asking about:
```

Carry this into the full vetting memo in the skill once they reply.
