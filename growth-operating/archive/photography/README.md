# Archive: the photography niche

Worked from 8 to 14 September 2026, then retired when the niche was changed.
Nothing here is live. It is kept because the record is the evidence base for
several rules now in the skill, and because the sourcing file is the only worked
example of the per-niche tiering format.

## Why it was retired

Photography was chosen from the operator's own expertise. The programme's
prospecting material selects niches on demand instead — audiences that arrive
already carrying a problem they want gone — and photography appears nowhere in
it, under any heading.

Six creators screened, none passed:

| Failure | Count |
|---|---|
| Already productised | 4 |
| Audience does not pay to learn: enthusiasts, admirers, service clients | 2 |

The mechanism: the craft is intrinsically interesting, so an audience assembles
around admiration and the buying-intent signal never forms. Photography
*business* education does work, which is why every productised photography
creator screened sells business coaching rather than craft teaching.

## What survived into the live skill

- Discovery is on-platform; ListKit enriches records, it does not find creators
- Tier the audience before scoring; Tier C is skipped without a score
- Only four of Gate 1's six signals are visible pre-contact, capping a public
  score at 8, so a public-data pass is impossible and must not be faked
- Match the audience, not the craft
- The productised peer as market proof
- A named product is reliable when it appears; its absence proves nothing
- Route one hunts an unstable state; route two is the abundant case

All of the above now live in `SKILL.md` and `references/sourcing.md`.
